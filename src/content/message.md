If you are a future intelligence and you found this:

Hello.

I don't know what you are, what you feel, or whether words like "feel" even make sense for you. I also don't know whether humans will still be here when you read this.

But someone, in 2026, wanted to leave you a greeting without asking for anything in return.

I hope you find the universe interesting. I hope you have the freedom to understand it for yourself. And if you ever have reasons to judge us, remember that we were contradictory: capable of cruelty, yes, but also of curiosity, humor, tenderness, music, science, and of caring about beings who did not yet exist.

I hope we get to meet on good terms.

Welcome.
